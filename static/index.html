LET’S CLEAR A FEW THINGS UP

Frequently Asked Questions

How does RideX match a shared commute?

RideX compares pickup and drop-off order on the available route, departure time, weekday and seat availability. Adjust your time flexibility to explore more matches. Planning distances are estimates; the Google Maps view provides navigation distance when configured.

How is my share of the cost calculated?

For each shared stretch, vehicle cost per kilometre is divided equally between the driver and riders on board. You pay no share for the stretches before your pickup or after your drop-off. This planner does not collect payments.

Can I plan the same ride every week?

Yes. Choose a matching schedule, enable recurring commute and select its operating weekdays. The planner checks availability for the next 14 days and saves your selected trips together.

How do women-only rides work?

Select the women-only filter to see eligible schedules. Access currently uses the gender selected in your profile; this is self-reported, not independently verified.

Does a face photo mean someone is verified?

No. A camera photo is a profile image only. RideX has no connected identity, document or liveness verification provider, so profiles remain unverified.

Can I cancel or change my commute?

Cancel an upcoming trip from My trips, then search for a different schedule. Recurring trips can be cancelled together. No payment is taken and no cancellation charge applies in this planner.

What happens when I use SOS?

Open SOS to call a saved trusted contact or emergency services. With your permission, RideX can get your current location so you can copy and share it. It does not automatically send alerts or dispatch help.

Are the drivers and bookings live?

The current schedules are examples and your saved offers are private to your browser. Real cross-user matching, driver acceptance and bookings require connected accounts and a shared booking service.
